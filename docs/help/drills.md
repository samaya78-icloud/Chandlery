Drills, safety committee and toolbox meetings: what is due, who took part, what was found, and the actions that follow.

## Getting started
1. Office (admin): set up the [Drill and meeting types](/drills/types): how often each is due and who must take part.
2. On board, look at the [Drill schedule](/drills) to see what is due, then use [Record a drill](/drills/new) after holding one: who took part and what was found.
3. For a safety meeting use [Record a meeting](/meetings/new), with its agenda, minutes and actions.
4. The office watches [Fleet drill status](/drills/fleet) and [Crew participation](/drills/crew).

## Pages
- [Drill schedule](/drills): each drill type with its status (Not due, Due soon, Due, Overdue, Never held). On board, [Record a drill](/drills/new).
- [Drill history](/drills/history): every drill held.
- [Crew participation](/drills/crew) (office): per crew member, when they last took part in each drill type.
- [Fleet drill status](/drills/fleet) (office): which vessels are behind.
- [Drill and meeting types](/drills/types) (admin): intervals, warning periods and who must take part.
- [Meetings](/meetings): safety committee, toolbox talks, briefings. On board, [Record a meeting](/meetings/new).

## Rules
A due date is the last drill of that type plus its interval ("monthly" means the end of the calendar month). Participation is worked out from the drills recorded, never typed. When more than a quarter of the crew has not taken part in the previous month's drills, drills are held within 24 hours of leaving port. Two records of one type within an hour are flagged as a possible duplicate.

## Findings and actions
Findings from a drill, and each meeting action (owner and due date), become [Worklist](/worklist) tasks. An action past its date is carried forward to the next meeting and marked as carried over.

## Linking drills and meetings to other records
Findings from a drill, and every meeting action (with an owner and due date), become [Worklist](/worklist) tasks. An action past its date is carried over to the next meeting.
