Periodic and one-off forms (engine performance, inspections, test records, checklists) that the office designs once and a vessel fills in offline.

## Getting started
1. Office (admin): open [Form types](/forms/types) and choose "+ New form". Design it from sections, fields and grids, set its periodicity and which vessels it applies to, then "Publish". To reuse an old HTML form, start with [Convert a legacy form](/forms/convert).
2. On board, the form appears on the vessel's [Forms](/forms) due list. Open the entry, fill it in (drafts save as you go) and submit.
3. The office accepts or returns it from [Submitted forms](/forms/review); watch what is overdue on [Forms due](/forms/due).

## Pages
- [Forms](/forms): the vessel's due list and its entries. Open an entry to fill it; drafts save as you go, then submit.
- [Forms due](/forms/due) (office): what is due and overdue by vessel.
- [Submitted forms](/forms/review) (office): accept or return with comments; raise action items that become Worklist tasks; print or export.
- [The year at a glance](/forms/year): which vessels have done which form.
- [Form types](/forms/types) (admin): the form designer. [Convert a legacy form](/forms/convert) turns an existing HTML form into a draft.

## How it works
A form type has a periodicity (daily to yearly, voyage, PMS, third party) and applies to vessel types or named vessels. A form is designed from sections, typed fields and grids, with rules (required, show if, validation, calculation, flag). Publishing creates a version; editing later creates a new version, and entries already filled stay on the version they were filled on.

## Review
A submitted entry has the form, comments and action items. Accepted entries are locked. Vessel and office check the entry against the same rules, so they cannot disagree.

## Linking forms to other records
Action items raised while reviewing a submitted form become [Worklist](/worklist) tasks.
