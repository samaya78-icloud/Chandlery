Anyone on board can report an incident, near miss, unsafe act or condition, or an observation. Every report reaches the Master, then the office, and is investigated in proportion to how serious it was or could have been.

## Getting started
1. Office (admin): check [Incident settings](/incidents/settings): the report types, root-cause categories and the risk matrix.
2. Anyone on board can start with [Report an incident](/incidents/new): choose the type, say what happened, the immediate action, and the actual and potential severity. A draft is visible only to you and the Master.
3. Submit it. The Master reviews it and sends it to the office, which investigates from the [Investigation queue](/incidents/queue).
4. Corrective and preventive actions are followed up until the report can be closed.

## Pages
- [Reports](/incidents): the list. [Report an incident](/incidents/new) starts a new one; a draft is visible only to the reporter and the Master.
- [Investigation queue](/incidents/queue) (office): reports waiting for investigation.
- [Statistics](/incidents/statistics) (office): counts and rates by vessel, type and period.
- [Lessons learned](/incidents/lessons): shared learning from closed reports.
- [Incident settings](/incidents/settings) (admin): report types, root-cause categories and the risk matrix used.

## Lifecycle
Draft, Reported, Reviewed (the Master confirms the facts and severity and sends it to the office), Investigating, Actions open, Closed. A serious report reaches the office at once.

## A report
Type, when and where, ship status, description, immediate action, persons involved, equipment, and both the actual and the potential severity on the company's risk matrix. A near miss has no actual harm but a high potential; that pairing is what makes the data useful.

## Investigation and actions
The investigation records facts, immediate cause, root causes and contributing factors. Each corrective or preventive action is a [Worklist](/worklist) task with an owner and a due date, and the report is not closed until they are done. A preventive action can be addressed to a group or the whole fleet.

## Linking incidents to other records
Each corrective or preventive action is a [Worklist](/worklist) task with an owner and due date, and the report stays open until they are done. A preventive action can go to a group of vessels or the whole fleet.
