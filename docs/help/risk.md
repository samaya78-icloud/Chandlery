Risk assessments are written from the office's library, signed off by the right person, locked once signed, and re-reviewed on a schedule.

## Getting started
1. Office (admin): in the [Risk library](/company/risk-library), make sure the templates and the risk matrix suit your company.
2. Start from [New assessment](/risk/new), choose a template, and fill in the job, team, activities and hazards with their controls.
3. Submit it. The Master or Chief Engineer on board, or a superintendent ashore, signs it from the [Sign-off queue](/risk/review).
4. After signing it is locked; a repeat of a recurring job starts with "Copy as interim".

## Pages
- [Assessments](/risk): all assessments with status and review dates. Start one with [New assessment](/risk/new).
- [Sign-off queue](/risk/review): assessments waiting for your signature.
- [Risk library](/company/risk-library) (admin): the reusable templates, versioned by the office, and the risk matrix.

## An assessment
Job description, category, team, work activities, and for each activity its hazards: effect, risk before controls, existing and additional controls, risk after controls, who is responsible and when. Risk is consequence (1 to 5) times probability (1 to 5) on the company's matrix, giving a band from Low to Extreme. Control actions with an owner and due date become Worklist tasks.

## Lifecycle
Draft, Submitted, Signed (locked), Interim (a repeat of a recurring job), Annual (re-reviewed after the review period, default 11 months), Superseded. After signing, a change creates a new version and the old one stays readable. "Copy as interim" starts from the last signed version.

## Sign-off
The Master or Chief Engineer signs on board, a superintendent ashore. If the company setting is on, the person who wrote an assessment cannot be the one who signs it when another reviewer is available.

## Linking risk assessments to other records
- Each control action with an owner and due date becomes a [Worklist](/worklist) task.
- A critical maintenance job may need a signed risk assessment before it can be completed (a company setting). See the [Plan](/pms).
