Structured inspections of a vessel's tanks (coating, structure, fittings), done on board, reviewed ashore, with ratings tracked over time.

## Getting started
1. Office (admin): check the [Tank checklists](/company/tank-checklists) for each tank type.
2. Open [Tanks](/tanks) and add the vessel's tanks with "+ Add tank", then lay them out on the tank plan. The office can copy a finished plan to sister vessels with [Copy to sister vessels](/tanks/copy).
3. Start your first inspection from [New inspection](/tanks/inspections/new), rate each criterion, and "Submit for review".
4. The office accepts or returns it from the [Review queue](/tanks/review).

## Pages
- [Tanks](/tanks): the tank master and the visual tank plan. Each tank is a shape coloured by type or latest rating; resize it by its edges and corners. Open a tank for its structural data and its rating trend.
- [Inspections](/tanks/inspections): the list of inspections. Start one from [New inspection](/tanks/inspections/new).
- [Exceptions](/tanks/exceptions): every criterion rated Fair or Bad, still open.
- [Review queue](/tanks/review) (office): submitted inspections waiting to be accepted or returned.
- [Copy to sister vessels](/tanks/copy) (office): copy a tank plan to sister vessels.
- [Tank checklists](/company/tank-checklists) (admin): the checklist templates by tank type.

## Doing an inspection
Pick the tank and date, say where it was done (at sea or port) and what work (internal, six-monthly). Rate each criterion (Good, Fair, Bad, NI not inspected, NA), add comments and photos, link the PMS job it came from, and submit.

## Lifecycle
Draft, Submitted, In review, then Reviewed (locked) or Returned for correction. Exceptions can become Worklist tasks. Ratings over time are shown as trends per tank.

## Linking tanks to other records
- While filling in an inspection, link the PMS job it came from.
- An exception (a criterion rated Fair or Bad) can become a [Worklist](/worklist) task with an owner and a due date.
