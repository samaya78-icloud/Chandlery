The Worklist is one recorded list of everything a vessel owes: audit and class findings, port state control items, defects, recommendations, deferred maintenance and internal tasks. Each task keeps a full progress history until it is completed.

## Getting started
1. Open the [Worklist](/worklist) and choose "+ New task". Give it a title, a source (who raised it), a nature, a priority and a target date.
2. Give it an owner (assignee) and department so it appears in their list.
3. Work it with progress entries: a comment, percent complete, a new date with a reason, or an attachment.
4. Mark it complete with evidence. The office confirms where verification is needed.
Tasks raised by other modules (a PMS defect, an incident action, a drill finding) arrive here already linked. The office sees every vessel on the [Fleet worklist](/worklist/fleet).

## Pages
- [Worklist](/worklist): the vessel's tasks (today, this week, overdue) with filters by source, nature, department and priority. Open a task to see its timeline.
- [Fleet worklist](/worklist/fleet): office view across vessels and groups.
- [Reports](/worklist/reports): overdue, ageing, completion rate, by source; export.
- [Worklist lists](/company/worklist-lists) (admin): the configurable lists for source, nature, categories and priorities.

## A task
Code, title, description, source (who raised it), nature (planned, emergency, deferred, survey, recommendation, condition), categories, priority, status (open, in progress, waiting, completed, cancelled), planned and target date, assignee and department, and links (a PMS job, a risk assessment, a requisition, an incident, a tank exception, a project item).

## Working a task
- Add progress with a comment, percent complete, a date change (with a reason) or an attachment. Entries are appended, never overwritten.
- Defer: give a reason and a new target date.
- Completed: the vessel marks it complete with evidence; the office confirms where verification is needed.
- Other modules create tasks for you: incident actions, drill findings, permit follow-ups, tank exceptions and PMS deferrals appear here with a link back.

## Who can change what
A task created on the vessel can have its priority, target date and assignee adjusted by the office; both sides add timeline entries.
