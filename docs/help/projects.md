Planned yard and technical projects (drydocks, repairs, retrofits) with schedule, specification, budget versus actual cost, documents and approvals. Actual costs come from purchase orders and invoices tagged to the project.

## Getting started
1. Start from [New project](/projects/new) (for people allowed to manage projects): name, vessel, type (drydock, repair, retrofit) and dates.
2. Add the specification items, then the budget lines and contingency.
3. Tender to yards, compare the quotes, and award; the approved quote becomes the baseline. The project then goes through approval.
4. During the stay record daily progress and variation orders; at the end reconcile actual cost against budget and close it.
Use [Project groups](/projects/groups) to group related projects and the [Fleet view](/projects/fleet) to watch them across vessels.

## Lifecycle
Planning, submitted for approval, Approved, Ongoing, Complete, then Closed after cost reconciliation. Budgets, variations and extensions go through the approval matrix; if the company setting is on and the project has a superintendent, that person's own sign-off is needed as well.

## Building a project
Add specification items (from deferred-to-drydock [Worklist](/worklist) tasks, due maintenance jobs, class requirements or owner requests), set budget lines and contingency, tender to yards and compare quotes, then award. The approved quote becomes the baseline. During the stay record daily progress and variation orders. At close, reconcile actual against budget; items done at the yard update Maintenance and the Worklist.

## Vessels
The vessel adds specification requests, progress notes and photos. Most of the project is owned by the office.

## Linking projects to other records
- Specification items come from deferred-to-drydock [Worklist](/worklist) tasks, due maintenance jobs on the [Plan](/pms), class requirements or owner requests.
- Actual cost comes from purchase orders and invoices tagged to the project.
- Items done at the yard update Maintenance and the Worklist when the project closes.
