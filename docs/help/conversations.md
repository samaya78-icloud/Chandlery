Every record in the product (requisition, PO, task, certificate, permit and so on) has its own conversation thread, so questions stay with the record instead of in email.

## Getting started
1. Open any record (a requisition, PO, task, certificate, permit or other) and find its conversation thread.
2. Write your message, attach photos or PDFs if needed, and send it. Choose internal, or shared where the record is shared with a supplier.
3. Everything waiting for you is in your [Conversations](/conversations) inbox, with unread counts.

## Pages
- [Conversations](/conversations): your inbox of threads with unread counts.

## Using a thread
Open the thread on a record and write a message; attach photos or PDFs. Messages are appended, so the vessel and the office never overwrite each other, and on a vessel they show their state: saved on ship, sent, received. System messages (status changes, approvals, deliveries) appear in the thread so it reads as the story of the record.

## Who sees what
A message is internal (company users, vessel and office) or, where the record is shared with a supplier (an RFQ or PO), can be shared with them. Suppliers see only the shared messages.
