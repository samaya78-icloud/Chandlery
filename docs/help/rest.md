Every seafarer's hours of work and rest are recorded daily and checked automatically against the ILO 180, STCW and MLC limits.

## Getting started
1. Office: make sure each seafarer exists in [Crew](/company/crew), and check the [Rules](/rest/rules) for the vessel's flag state or agreement.
2. On board: each person opens [My hours](/rest) and enters their work periods for the day (start and end to the quarter hour; everything else counts as rest).
3. Check the [Month sheet](/rest/sheet), then at month end sign on [Sign hours](/rest/sign): the seafarer first, then the Master.
4. The Master reviews any days listed under [Breaches](/rest/breaches). The office follows compliance on [Fleet compliance](/rest/fleet).
To bring in existing records, use [Import history](/rest/import).

## Pages (on board)
- [My hours](/rest): enter the work periods for a day (start and end to the quarter hour; everything else is rest). Corrections are new revisions with a reason.
- [Month sheet](/rest/sheet): the month in the model format, to review and print.
- [Sign hours](/rest/sign): the seafarer signs their month, then the Master (or someone the Master authorises).
- [Breaches](/rest/breaches): days that break a rule; each is explained and reviewed by the Master.
- [Working table](/rest/table): the table of shipboard working arrangements per position, to print and post.

## Pages (office)
[Fleet compliance](/rest/fleet), [Reports](/rest/reports), [Rules](/rest/rules) (the rule set, which a flag state or agreement can change), [Import history](/rest/import).

## Default rules
At least 10 hours of rest in any 24 hours and 77 hours in any 7 days; rest in at most two periods, one of at least 6 hours; no more than 14 hours between rest periods. The Master may record an exception for an emergency or drill, with a reason; it is flagged, not hidden.

## Good to know
The vessel's day status (at sea, in port, anchored) chooses the row of the working table that pre-fills a day. Records are never deleted; the office sees unsigned months and breaches.
